What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that counts the number of words in a piece of text. It can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators, and anyone who needs to meet a specific word limit.

Why Use a Word Counter?

A word counter can help you quickly check whether your article, essay, assignment, or other content meets its required length.
