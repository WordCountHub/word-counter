WORD COUNTER GUIDE

What Is a Word Counter?

A word counter is an online tool that calculates the number of words in a piece of text. Word counting is useful when writing essays, articles, blog posts, applications, reports, social media content, and other documents with specific length requirements.

WordCountHub also provides character, sentence, paragraph, reading-time, and speaking-time statistics so you can understand the length of your writing at a glance.

Why Use a Word Counter?

Many types of writing have recommended or required lengths. Keeping track of your word count can help you stay within an assignment requirement, article target, application limit, or other writing constraint.

Who Can Use a Word Counter?

Students can use a word counter to check essays and assignments. Writers and bloggers can use it to keep articles within a desired length. Professionals can use it for reports, applications, presentations, and other written material.
