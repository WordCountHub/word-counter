About WordCountHub

A simple, fast, and free online tool for counting words and understanding your writing.

What Is WordCountHub?

WordCountHub is a free online writing tool designed to make checking text length simple and convenient.

Whether you are writing an essay, article, blog post, report, application, or social media content, WordCountHub helps you quickly understand the size of your text.

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide straightforward online text tools that are easy to use without unnecessary registration, complicated interfaces, or distracting features.

What You Can Check

🔢

Word Count

See how many words are contained in your text.

🔤

Character Count

Check total characters and characters without spaces.

📝

Sentences & Paragraphs

Quickly see the number of sentences and paragraphs.

⏱️

Reading & Speaking Time

Get an estimated reading and speaking time for your text.

Who Can Use WordCountHub?

WordCountHub can be useful for students, writers, bloggers, content creators, professionals, teachers, and anyone who needs to check the length of written content.

Simple and Easy to Use

Just enter or paste your text into the word counter. The statistics update automatically, so you can see your results immediately.

Privacy-Friendly Design

The word counting calculations are performed directly in your browser. You do not need to create an account to use the basic word counter.
